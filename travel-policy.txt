TRAVEL ASSISTANT - TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. Cancellation and Refund Policy
If a ticket is cancelled 24 hours or more before departure, 80 percent of the ticket price is refunded.
If a ticket is cancelled less than 24 hours before departure, 50 percent of the ticket price is refunded.
No cancellation is possible after the flight has departed. Refunds are credited within 5 to 7 working days.

2. Baggage Policy
Each passenger can carry one cabin bag up to 7 kg and check-in baggage up to 15 kg free of charge.
Extra baggage is charged Rs. 500 per kg at the airport.

3. Check-in Rules
Web check-in opens 48 hours before departure and closes 2 hours before departure.
Passengers must reach the airport at least 2 hours before a domestic flight.

4. Passenger Details
The passenger name must match the government photo ID. Name, age and mobile number can be corrected
before departure through the assistant or the website. Seat changes are free of charge, subject to availability.

5. Hotel Booking Policy
Hotel bookings can be cancelled free of charge until the day before check-in.
Standard check-in time is 2 PM and check-out time is 11 AM.
